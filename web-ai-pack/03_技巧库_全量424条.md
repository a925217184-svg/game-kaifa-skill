**1. Slow Zoom In**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A slow zoom in is slow zoom in cinematography that tightens the frame by increasing focal length while the camera body stays locked. Perspective relations stay fixed: the world does not slide, it only enlarges as the lens compresses space.
- Prompt 模板：Slow optical zoom in on [Subject], camera body locked on a heavy tripod, lens gradually tightening over several seconds, background compression increasing, no physical dolly, prestige-drama patience. For motion: the lens slowly zooms in for 6 seconds while the camera body stays locked and the subject stays centered.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dolly In or a digital crop sold as a zoom. The tell is parallax: if a foreground chair slides against the wall, you translated.

**2. Slow Zoom Out**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A slow zoom out widens the frame by decreasing focal length while the support stays put. More architecture and negative space enter the picture, yet the camera never travels, so true parallax does not appear.
- Prompt 模板：Slow optical zoom out from [Subject], tripod locked, the frame breathing open to reveal surrounding architecture and negative space, no camera body travel. For motion: the lens slowly zooms out for 6 seconds from a tight frame on [Subject] into a wider environment.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dolly Out. The tell is a sliding foreground: plants, shoulders, or door jambs changing their overlap.

**3. Crash Zoom In**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A crash zoom is a rapid focal-length snap from a wider frame into a detail, usually without moving the camera. The punch is optical and conspicuous: size changes faster than a body can walk.
- Prompt 模板：A solemn auctioneer lifts a plain wooden box. Snap-zoom instantly from a medium shot to the tiny blinking light hidden under its lid. Fixed camera position, strong punchy zoom, immediate sharp landing, no forward dolly and no slow-motion.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a fast Dolly In or a Whip Pan. The tell is parallax or horizontal streaking instead of radial zoom blur.

**4. Crash Zoom Out**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A crash zoom out is a sudden, fast widening of the lens from a tight frame into a much wider one, usually without moving the camera. It dumps the subject back into the environment in a fraction of a second.
- Prompt 模板：Sudden crash zoom out from [Subject], the environment exploding into view in a fraction of a second, then a locked wide hold. For motion: snap zoom out from [Subject] in under a second, revealing the wider scene, then hold.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dolly Out or a Crash Zoom In fired in the wrong direction. The tell is sliding foreground or a punch that gets tighter instead of wider.

**5. Dolly In**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A dolly in shot moves the entire camera through space on a wheeled or tracked support, advancing toward the subject at a measured pace. The nodal point walks, so foreground and background shift in parallax as the surroundings are progressively excluded.
- Prompt 模板：An archivist sits at a desk and notices a familiar signature on an old letter. Begin in a medium-wide shot. Over eight seconds dolly slowly forward to a close-up, fixed lens, subtle foreground parallax, no zoom. Her eyes lift only at the end. Restrained daylight, no cut.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Slow Zoom In or a crop. The tell is missing parallax: walls grow but never slide.

**6. Dolly Out**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A dolly out shot withdraws the entire camera through space on a wheeled or tracked support. The world widens with true parallax, and the subject shrinks inside architecture because the station point has actually retreated.
- Prompt 模板：Start tightly on a calm pianist's face. Pull the camera backward in one uninterrupted shot, fixed focal length, gradually revealing that the grand piano stands alone in a flooded ballroom. Keep the face natural and delay the water reveal until the middle of the shot.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Slow Zoom Out. The tell is a flat grow of the frame with no new overlap of furniture.

**7. Dolly Left**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A dolly left, also called a truck left, slides the whole camera sideways to the left on wheels or track. The lens stays aimed with the subject while background planes peel past in parallax.
- Prompt 模板：Lateral dolly left across [Subject], camera sliding sideways on track, background planes peeling in parallax, subject tracked in frame. For motion: the camera slides left on a dolly, keeping [Subject] in frame, background parallax visible.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Pan Left. The tell is a rotating horizon of objects with no change of station point, and no peeling of near columns against far walls.

**8. Dolly Right**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A dolly right, or truck right, moves the camera body sideways to the right without panning. New space enters from one side of the frame while the other side exits, and layered parallax proves the translation.
- Prompt 模板：Lateral dolly right across [Subject], smooth sideways travel, layered parallax, locked horizon. For motion: the camera slides right on a dolly, keeping [Subject] framed, smooth lateral parallax.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Pan Right or an 360-Degree Orbit that starts to curve. The tell is yaw, a dipping horizon, or a changing camera-to-subject distance.

**9. Pan Left**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A pan left is pan shot cinematography that rotates the camera on a fixed tripod or head around its vertical axis, sweeping the view to the left. The support does not travel; only the direction of the lens changes.
- Prompt 模板：Slow pan left from a locked tripod across [Subject], camera rotating on its vertical axis, horizon level, revealing new architecture to the left. For motion: the camera pans left from a fixed tripod, sweeping across [Subject] and the environment.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dolly Left. The tell is missing parallax of near objects, or the opposite: you added a slide by accident.

**10. Pan Right**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A pan right shot is a horizontal rotation of a locked-off camera toward the right. It connects two parts of the same geography in one continuous turn from a single station point.
- Prompt 模板：Slow pan right from a locked tripod across [Subject], fluid head rotation, level horizon, a continuous horizontal reveal. For motion: the camera pans right from a fixed tripod across [Subject].
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dolly Right or a Whip Pan that never resolves. The tell is parallax you did not want, or blur that never hard-stops.

**11. Tilt Up**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A tilt up shot rotates the camera vertically around a fixed position, climbing from a lower composition to a higher one. The tripod does not rise; only pitch changes, so the viewpoint height stays the same.
- Prompt 模板：Slow tilt up from [Subject], camera rotating on its horizontal axis, climbing from ground detail to face or from face to towering architecture. For motion: the camera tilts up from a fixed position, starting low on [Subject] and climbing vertically.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Crane Up or Pedestal Up. The tell is a changing eyeline height and sliding parallax of near objects.

**12. Tilt Down**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A tilt-down rotates the camera vertically from a fixed position, starting higher and ending lower. It can drop from sky or architecture onto a person or object without moving the tripod.
- Prompt 模板：Slow tilt down onto [Subject], starting in sky or ceiling and descending to the figure, a measured vertical scan. For motion: the camera tilts down from above onto [Subject], revealing the figure from head to environment.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Crane Down. The tell is the camera's eyeline dropping through floors, with parallax as objects rise in frame.

**13. Whip Pan**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A whip pan pans so quickly that the image blurs into horizontal streaks, then snaps to a new composition. Two shots can be joined inside compatible directional motion blur, creating the impression of one fast reorientation.
- Prompt 模板：Generate A: a baker turns toward a ringing telephone and the camera whip-pans right into horizontal blur. Generate B: begin with the same rightward blur and land on a flour-covered delivery driver outside. Join at maximum blur. Keep motion direction and brightness compatible; no dissolve.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a fast Pan Right that never blurs, or a Crash Zoom In. The tell is readable detail in the middle, radial zoom blur, or a seam where blur directions disagr…

**14. Whip Tilt**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A whip tilt is a violent, motion-blurred vertical rotation of the camera, then a hard stop. It is the up-or-down cousin of a whip pan: shock through speed, not through traveling the support.
- Prompt 模板：Violent vertical whip tilt across [Subject], the frame ripping up or down with motion blur, then a hard stop. For motion: extremely fast vertical whip tilt across [Subject] with motion blur, then a hard stop.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Whip Pan or a fast Tilt Down that remains readable. The tell is horizontal streaks, or a clear building in the middle of the move.

**15. Pedestal Up**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A pedestal shot moves the entire camera vertically without changing its tilt. The lens rises on a column, eyeline climbs, and architecture slides down the frame while pitch stays constant.
- Prompt 模板：Pedestal up on [Subject], camera rising vertically on a column with no tilt change, eyeline climbing, architecture sliding down the frame. For motion: the camera rises straight up on a pedestal, keeping the same tilt, lifting past [Subject].
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Tilt Up or a Crane Up. The tell is a pitching horizon, or a backward boom that reveals a city.

**16. Pedestal Down**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A pedestal down lowers the entire camera vertically without changing its tilt. The descent stays on one axis: a calm drop through the same look, not a nod and not a crane swoop.
- Prompt 模板：Pedestal down on [Subject], camera lowering vertically, no tilt, a calm descent through the same axis. For motion: the camera lowers straight down on a pedestal, keeping tilt constant, settling onto [Subject].
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Tilt Down or Crane Down. The tell is a nodding horizon or a curved path over furniture.

**17. Crane Up**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A crane shot lifts the camera on a jib or Technocrane, often easing backward at the same time, so the frame climbs from a human scale into a wider view of the location. Height change is the point, and a little boom-out keeps the subject from sliding off the bottom of the frame.
- Prompt 模板：Majestic crane up from [Subject], camera rising and easing backward on a Technocrane, the individual becoming one figure inside a vast environment, golden or blue-hour air, 35mm Signature Prime. For motion: the camera cranes smoothly upward and slightly backward from shoulder height, rising above [Subject] to reveal the true surrounding scale.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Tilt Up or a cut from close-up to aerial. The tell is no parallax, or a jump in scale with no continuous ascent.

**18. Crane Down**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A crane down shot lowers the camera on an arm from a high wide view into a human-scale frame. The world collapses from map to face because the lens occupies new heights, not because it merely nods.
- Prompt 模板：Begin high over a lively harbor market. Descend beside a woman unloading oranges, then continue at her shoulder as she walks between stalls. The high-to-ground movement feels continuous with no sudden speed or horizon change. Plan separate crane and walking sections joined at a foreground canopy if needed.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Tilt Down or a cut from aerial to close-up. The tell is a nodding camera that never loses altitude, or a scale jump.

**19. Crane Over**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A crane-over lifts and arcs the camera on a jib or crane until it looks down on the scene from above. Height and angle change together in one move, turning the floor into a map.
- Prompt 模板：Crane over [Subject], the arm rising and arcing forward until the camera looks nearly straight down, a continuous climb into overhead. For motion: the camera cranes up and over [Subject], finishing in a high downward angle.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Crane Up that never looks down, or a cut to Overhead Top-Down. The tell is a forward skyline at the end, or a jump to top-down.

**20. Jib Shot**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A jib shot uses a short pivoting arm to raise, lower, or sweep the camera a few feet. It is a compact crane: small height change, often over a table, a walk, or a two-shot.
- Prompt 模板：Short jib move on [Subject], a compact crane arm lifting or sweeping a few feet, more intimate than a full Technocrane, elegant vertical accent. For motion: a compact jib arm lifts a few feet above [Subject] with a gentle sweep.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a full Crane Up or a Pedestal Up. The tell is a skyline, or a perfectly vertical column with no arc.

**21. 360-Degree Orbit**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A 360-degree orbit shot circles the subject through a full turn, usually at a constant radius, always facing them. The background becomes a continuous ribbon while the person stays the center of interest.
- Prompt 模板：Full 360-degree orbit around [Subject], camera circling at constant radius, background becoming a continuous ribbon of light, centripetal romantic or predatory energy. For motion: the camera orbits 360 degrees around [Subject], always facing them, background wrapping as a ribbon of light.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a subject rotating on the spot, or a Pan Left from sticks. The tell is a fixed background with a spinning body, or no parallax.

**22. Arc Left**（镜头运动）
- 定义：An arc left is a curved dolly that travels partway around the subject while staying aimed at them. The camera's axis on the conversation or room changes without a cut, usually about a quarter-orbit.
- Prompt 模板：90-degree arc left around [Subject], curved dolly, always facing the figure, background sliding, a quarter-orbit instead of a full spin. For motion: the camera arcs left about 90 degrees around [Subject], always facing them.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dolly Left or a full 360-Degree Orbit. The tell is a straight track that loses the subject, or a spin that never stops.

**23. Arc Right**（镜头运动）
- 定义：An arc right is a partial orbit to the right, usually about a quarter turn, with the lens remaining on the subject. Background slides while eyeline and distance stay roughly constant.
- Prompt 模板：90-degree arc right around [Subject], curved travel, continuous eyeline on the figure. For motion: the camera arcs right about 90 degrees around [Subject], always facing them.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dolly Right or 360-Degree Orbit. The tell is a straight slide that changes distance, or a spin past the new fact.

**24. Tracking Shot**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A tracking shot moves with, beside, ahead of, or behind a moving subject, typically matching pace on a parallel path. The person supplies the motion; the camera keeps distance, height, and scale while foreground, mid-ground, and deep background peel.
- Prompt 模板：Side-on continuous shot of a tired security guard trying to pass three obstructing figures in a narrow service corridor. Choreographed non-graphic action, visible pauses and fatigue, full bodies readable, camera tracks only laterally. No rapid edits, no impossible acrobatics, no concealed geography.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Pan Left from a locked head, or a Slow Zoom In that only changes size. The tell is no sliding foreground.

**25. Follow Shot**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A follow shot trails a moving subject at a fixed offset, usually from behind. The back of the head becomes foreground and the world opens ahead, so pursuit, haunting, or arrival is felt as walking into space.
- Prompt 模板：Follow shot behind [Subject], camera trailing at a fixed offset, the back of the head as foreground, the world opening ahead. For motion: the camera follows behind [Subject], matching their movement through the space.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a side Tracking Shot shot or a SnorriCam. The tell is a full profile, or a pinned face with a sliding world.

**26. Steadicam**（镜头运动）
- 定义：Steadicam is a mechanical stabilizing system using a sled, arm, and vest, so an operator can walk while the camera floats. The path can connect rooms with a smoothness that is neither handheld tremor nor dolly traction.
- Prompt 模板：Steadicam floating with [Subject] through connected spaces, preternatural smoothness, operator walking, light temperatures shifting as rooms change. For motion: smooth Steadicam move floating with [Subject] through the environment, no footfall shake.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is Handheld shake added as a preset, or a Gimbal Shot clip that never feels like a vest. The tell is tremor, or a weightless electronic drift with no human g…

**27. Handheld**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A handheld camera is operated from the body without a stabilizing vest or gimbal, so breathing, micro-shake, and mass stay in the frame. The image has the nerves of a person holding weight, not a track and not a motor.
- Prompt 模板：Inside a moving minibus, a compact camera pivots from two chatting passengers to the windscreen as a fallen tree blocks the road, then to the driver's reaction and the rear window. One continuous-looking viewpoint, consistent seats, believable window parallax, restrained panic. Build separate interior and exterior plates when necessary.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a shake preset on a Gimbal Shot or Static Locked-Off clip. The tell is wobble without mass, or a perfectly level horizon with fake jitter.

**28. Gimbal Shot**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A gimbal shot uses motorized stabilization for smooth handheld or vehicle-mounted movement. Electronic inertia damps roll and high-frequency shake while a human still carries the camera, freer than a dolly and cleaner than raw handheld.
- Prompt 模板：Gimbal move with [Subject], electronically stabilized but still human-carried, smoother than handheld, freer than dolly, modern action grammar. For motion: smooth gimbal move following [Subject], stabilized but slightly human, gliding through the space.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is Handheld shake or a full Steadicam vest sentence. The tell is tremor, or a claim of Steadicam when the image has that weightless motor lock.

**29. FPV Drone**（镜头运动）
- 定义：An FPV drone shot is first-person aerial flying: the camera banks, dives, and threads architecture at high speed. It is proximity and flight, a racing craft with a lens, not a slow crane and not a high establishing hover.
- Prompt 模板：FPV drone racing past [Subject], first-person flight, banking turns, proximity to architecture, motion blur on edges, extreme sports camera path. For motion: FPV drone flies past and around [Subject] with banking turns and proximity to structures.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a slow Aerial Pullback or a Crane Up. The tell is a level, hovering horizon and no bank.

**30. Aerial Pullback**（镜头运动）
- 定义：An aerial pullback rises and recedes until a person or building becomes a small mark in a landscape. The move hands the scene from a character to geography, from helicopter or high-drone height rather than from a ground crane.
- Prompt 模板：Aerial pullback from [Subject], camera rising and receding until the figure is a speck in geography, helicopter or high drone grammar, landscape taking the frame. For motion: the camera rises and pulls back from [Subject] until the surrounding landscape dominates.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Crane Up or a cut to an Extreme Long Shot. The tell is a jib-sized lift, or a jump in scale.

**31. Aerial Push In**（镜头运动）
- 定义：An aerial push in starts high and descends or tightens until a single figure reads in the map. It picks one human out of a city, crowd, or terrain from altitude.
- Prompt 模板：Aerial push in toward [Subject] from high altitude, descending and tightening from map to person, a high view finding a face. For motion: the camera descends and pushes in from a high aerial toward [Subject].
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Crane Down or a Slow Zoom In on a wide still. The tell is jib height, or optical enlargement with no descent.

**32. Push In**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A push in shot moves physically or virtually closer to the subject so size grows because the camera walks. It is a short pressure advance, often so gradual the audience feels the walls close before they notice motion.
- Prompt 模板：Imperceptibly slow push in on [Subject], the camera closing from medium to medium close-up so gradually the audience feels walls closing before they notice motion. For motion: very slow push in toward [Subject] over 6 seconds, medium into medium close-up.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Slow Zoom In. The tell is enlargement without sliding foreground.

**33. Pull Out**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A pull out shot moves physically or virtually away from the subject so more environment enters around a person or detail. Intimacy widens into context without changing lenses.
- Prompt 模板：Slow pull out from [Subject], intimacy widening into environment, the person remaining while the world claims them. For motion: slow pull out from [Subject], the frame widening to reveal the environment.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Slow Zoom Out. The tell is no parallax as the room grows.

**34. Static Locked-Off**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A static locked-off shot plants the camera on a heavy tripod with zero camera movement. Composition, blocking, and duration do the work; the frame is a living picture that does not tour.
- Prompt 模板：In a dark archive, a librarian listens for someone outside. Remove music entirely and retain only quiet ventilation, cloth movement and her restrained breathing. Let the ventilation stop, leaving a single distant latch click clearly exposed. No sudden monster reveal or oversized bass hit.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a tiny Slider Shot or a breathing Handheld. The tell is any parallax or micro-pan.

**35. Dolly Zoom**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A dolly zoom combines camera movement and opposite zooming to preserve subject size while distorting perspective. It is not a zoom with a digital background stretch: translation and focal length work against each other.
- Prompt 模板：One continuous six-second shot of a station attendant realizing a train is missing. Dolly straight backward while optically zooming in at the compensating rate. Keep her head exactly the same height and centered; the long platform behind her appears to compress. She barely moves. Stable facial identity, rigid architecture, no cut, no ordinary push-in.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Slow Zoom In or a background warp in post. The tell is a changing subject size, or a stretch with no camera travel.

**36. Dutch Roll**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A Dutch roll rotates the camera around the lens axis over time, so a level horizon becomes a canted one. Unease arrives as a move, not as a frozen Dutch still.
- Prompt 模板：Slow roll of the camera on its lens axis around [Subject], horizon rotating from level into a Dutch angle, the world itself tilting. For motion: the camera slowly rolls, tilting the horizon around [Subject] into a Dutch angle.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a locked Dutch Angle or a full Camera Roll. The tell is a still tilt from frame one, or a continuous spin past 45 degrees with no stop.

**37. Camera Roll**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A camera roll rotates the frame around the lens axis as if the world has lost its up, either by rolling the camera or by rotating a room around a weighted body. Gravity becomes optional, which is stronger than a small Dutch cant.
- Prompt 模板：A rigid hotel corridor slowly rolls ninety degrees while a courier braces against a wall that becomes the floor. Maintain coherent gravity, body weight, cloth motion and contact. Previsualize the room rotation in 3D, then generate short connected beats from fixed endpoints. No bending walls or effortless floating.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Dutch Angle or an 360-Degree Orbit. The tell is a still cant, or a camera circling while the horizon stays mostly level.

**38. Double Dolly**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A double dolly shot moves performer and camera together on one rolling platform so their relative arrangement stays fixed. The actor appears to glide rather than walk: feet skating, weight transfer absent.
- Prompt 模板：A detective stands completely still, feet planted, while she and the camera glide forward together down a long corridor. Her face stays the same size and position. Background moves smoothly backward. No walking cycle, no bobbing, no flying pose, solemn inevitability.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Steadicam walk or a solo Dolly In. The tell is a normal gait, or a planted actor growing in frame.

**39. SnorriCam**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A SnorriCam fixes the camera to the performer's body so the body remains stable relative to frame while the world moves. A rig points back at the wearer: the face stays pinned, the environment pitches around it.
- Prompt 模板：A frightened commuter walks quickly through a tiled tunnel. Body-mounted camera facing him: face and shoulders stay locked in the center while the corridor tilts and bobs with every step. Wide-angle proximity, natural strain, no free camera orbit and no normal handheld tracking.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is Point of View or a Follow Shot from behind. The tell is seeing the street as the actor would, or seeing their back.

**40. Locked-On**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A locked-on shot keeps one object or face glued in frame while the world streams past, as if the camera were bolted to a vehicle, a fall, or a spinning set. The subject is the only stable point.
- Prompt 模板：Locked-on move with [Subject], camera glued to one object or face while the environment streams past, as if bolted to a vehicle or a falling body. For motion: the camera stays locked on [Subject] while the environment streams past them.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a Follow Shot or SnorriCam. The tell is a walking offset behind a person, or a chest rig whose job is the face as tripod for a sidewalk.

**41. Walk and Talk**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A walk and talk shot moves with speaking people, camera leading or flanking at medium, so information and geography travel together. Architecture flows while a moving two-shot (or a moving single with interruptions) carries the dialogue.
- Prompt 模板：Walk-and-talk tracking of [Subject] in conversation motion, camera leading or flanking at medium, architecture flowing, West Wing corridor energy. For motion: the camera leads [Subject] walking through a space, holding a moving medium two-shot energy even if one figure.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a silent Follow Shot or a seated two-shot with a slow Dolly In. The tell is the back of a head, or feet that do not walk.

**42. Hyperlapse**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A hyperlapse combines time-lapse with significant camera movement through space. Time is compressed while the camera advances in steps that assemble into a glide, so crowds and weather race along a real path.
- Prompt 模板：Hyperlapse toward or around [Subject], time compressed, crowds and weather racing, camera advancing in steps that become a glide. For motion: hyperlapse: the camera advances through the space around [Subject] while time is compressed, clouds and traffic racing.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a locked Time-Lapse or a real-time Tracking Shot shot with a speed ramp. The tell is a still station point, or real-time gait.

**43. Slider Shot**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A slider shot is a short, precise linear move on a compact rail, often 60 to 120 centimeters. It adds parallax to an interview, product, or lock-off without a full dolly track.
- Prompt 模板：Short slider move across [Subject], 60 to 120 centimeters of lateral travel, product or interview parallax, precise and small. For motion: a short slider move laterally across [Subject], subtle parallax, 6 seconds.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a full Dolly Left or a tiny 360-Degree Orbit. The tell is meters of travel, or a curve.

**44. Lazy Susan**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A lazy susan shot rotates the subject on a motorized turntable while the camera stays put, or the camera circles at a fixed product-hero radius. Distance holds, so scale does not change, and the far wall barely shears if the disc is what turns.
- Prompt 模板：Quote the move, not a mood. Still: Lazy-Susan rotation of [Subject] on a turntable while the camera stays put, or the camera circles a static product, commercial 360 hero.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is an orbit. The tell: background sliding, a lamp shearing, the room turning.

**45. Bolt Cam**（镜头运动）
- 定义：A bolt cam hurls an ultra-high-speed camera along a crash rig toward an impact, then freezes. The punch is physical translation on a rail or pneumatic throw, not a change of focal length.
- Prompt 模板：Name the strike, the rig, and the freeze. Still: Bolt-cam strike toward [Subject], ultra-high-speed camera on a crash rig, the lens hurled at an impact then freezing, sports and music-video violence.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is a crash zoom. The tell: no parallax, the background simply enlarges.

**46. Aerial**（镜头运动）
- 定义：An aerial shot views a large area from a high flying camera so geography reads as a map, the figure small in land or city. Helicopter, drone, or a virtual path can carry it; altitude and a stable horizon are the facts.

**99. Trunk Shot**（机位角度）
- 定义：A trunk shot places the camera inside a car trunk or similar confined pit, looking up at the people who open it, with the lid and rim framing their downward looks. It is a located low viewpoint, not a generic look-up in a parking lot.
- Prompt 模板：From inside an old wooden instrument case, look upward as two conservators open the lid and lean over it. The dark case rim frames their faces against a bright workshop ceiling. Clear lid movement, coherent downward eyelines, no camera rise or reveal of the observer.

**100. Hip Level**（机位角度）
- 定义：A hip-level shot sits the camera around belt or holster height so hands, gait, and weapons share the plane of focus. It is a height choice, usually with a level horizon: the hands will decide.

**101. Shoulder Level**（机位角度）
- 定义：A shoulder-level camera sits slightly below the eyes, at the height of a walking companion. It is less formal than true Eye Level and less monumental than a Low Angle; the face is still the target, seen from the deltoid rather than the pupil.
- Prompt 模板：Separate height from move. Quote: Shoulder-level camera on [Subject], slightly below the eyes, a walking companion's view, less formal than true eye-level.

**102. First-Person**（机位角度）

**104. Object POV**（机位角度）

**105. Incline**（机位角度）

**109. Medium Close-Up (MCU)**（构图景别）

**110. Medium Shot (MS)**（构图景别）

**111. Cowboy Shot**（构图景别）

**112. Full Body (WS)**（构图景别）

**123. Over-the-Shoulder Coverage (OTS)**（构图景别）

**124. Single**（构图景别）

**125. Cut-ins**（构图景别）

**196. Broad Lighting**（灯光）

**197. Silhouette**（灯光）

**202. Bounce Light**（灯光）

- 定义：A freeze frame holds one image so narrative time stops while camera, subject, and world all cease to generate new samples. The hold can end a story, land a joke, or present a face as evidence.
- Prompt 模板：Edit a group of exhausted friends leaving an all-night diner at sunrise to an original energetic rock cue. - 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is Frozen in Motion or Bullet Time. The tell is rain or coat tails hanging while the viewpoint keeps traveling, or a mid-air sculpture with parallax.

**244. Time-Lapse**（时间运动）

**245. Step Printing**（时间运动）

**246. Reverse Motion**（时间运动）

- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is reverse chronology or a Boomerang. The tell is a normal-speed scene shuffle, or a clip that goes forward then snaps back.

**247. Bullet Time**（时间运动）

**249. Oner**（时间运动）
- 定义：A oner shot is designed to play as if the scene were one take, with no visible edits. It may be a true single take or a stitched oner whose joins hide in whips, darkness, or crossing bodies.

**250. Motion Blur**（时间运动）
- 定义：Motion blur cinematography is the streaking or softness caused by subject or camera movement during exposure. The longer the gate stays open relative to the motion, the more the image paints, so edges fail on purpose.

**251. Stutter / Stop-Stutter**（时间运动）
- 定义：Stutter editing drops or repeats frames so playback skips and the world's clock hitches. It is an anxious rhythm, not the lyric smear of step printing and not a jump cut's spatial jump.
- Prompt 模板：Write frames dropping or repeating, not smear and not a jump in angle. Still: a face or gesture that looks temporally sampled, slight after-images only if they come from repeats.
- 避坑：The usual wrong cousin is Step Printing or Jump Cut. The tell is romantic long-exposure crowds, or a sudden change of body position that reads as an edit.

**252. Boomerang**（时间运动）

**253. Infinite Loop**（时间运动）

**254. Stop Motion**（时间运动）

**255. Low Shutter**（时间运动）
- 定义：Low shutter cinematography uses a short shutter angle or short exposure so moving edges stay sharp inside each frame. Normal-rate action then feels abrupt and staccato because this is reduced motion blur, not a long-exposure smear.

**256. Timelapse Glam**（时间运动）

**257. Timelapse Human**（时间运动）

**258. Timelapse Landscape**（时间运动）

**259. Frozen in Motion**（时间运动）

**260. Moonwalk**（时间运动）

**261. Lens Flare**（机内特效）
- 定义：Lens flare in lens flare cinematography is stray light scattered by glass, coatings, and barrel walls, producing ghosts, streaks, or veiling glare instead of a clean image. The artifacts live in the taking optic, so they track the source angle and dim when a body occludes the lam…

**262. Anamorphic Flare**（机内特效）
- 定义：An anamorphic flare is a horizontal streak produced when a bright point hits the cylindrical elements of an anamorphic lens. It is a subset of lens flare, not a general haze, and it usually arrives with oval bokeh from the same squeeze.
- Prompt 模板：State squeeze and streak axis. Example: Horizontal anamorphic flares streaking through [Subject], cyan or magenta bars from headlights or neon, oval bokeh, 2x squeeze, not round spherical ghosts.
- 避坑：Wrong cousin: generic lens flare or a spherical rainbow. Tell: round bokeh with a pasted horizontal bar that ignores source position.

**263. Halation**（机内特效）
- 定义：Halation in film is a reddish or warm glow around bright highlights caused by light passing through the emulsion, reflecting off the base, and scattering back into the image. It is a stock behavior, not a diffusion filter and not flare inside the lens.
- Prompt 模板：A woman reads beside a bright summer window. Fine facial detail stays readable while window highlights bloom gently into the surrounding air. Subtle soft filtration, luminous skin without smoothing away texture, no fog overlay or global defocus.
- 避坑：Wrong cousin: Promist, bloom filter, or shallow-focus mush. Tell: even blur, no red-edge, highlights crushed to white with no density.

**264. Film Grain**（机内特效）
- 定义：Film grain in film grain cinematography is the random texture of silver halide crystals or color dye clouds in the emulsion, or a digital simulation of that structure. It is a developed surface, not a PNG overlay sitting on top of a clean render.
- Prompt 模板：Specify format and that the grain is in the image, not on it. Example: Visible film grain over [Subject], 35mm or 16mm dye-cloud texture, temporally unstable, denser in shadows, not a cheap overlay.
- 避坑：Wrong cousin: a repeating overlay, digital noise, or halation bloom. Tell: identical grain on every frame, speckle in clipped whites, or a wallpaper that ignores motion.

**265. Bokeh**（机内特效）
- 定义：Bokeh in bokeh cinematography is the aesthetic character of out-of-focus rendering: the disc shape, edge, and smoothness of blurred points, not the mere fact of a thin depth of field. The glass, not a blur slider, decides whether those discs are round, oval, outlined, or swirling…

**266. Double Exposure**（机内特效）

**267. Rack Focus**（机内特效）

**268. Shallow Focus**（机内特效）

**269. Deep Focus**（机内特效）

**270. Forced Perspective**（机内特效）

**271. Light Leak**（机内特效）

**272. Vignette**（机内特效）

**273. Chromatic Aberration**（机内特效）

**274. Infrared**（机内特效）
